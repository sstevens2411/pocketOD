Key Points
  • Most soft lens families offer a single base curve and diameter — fitting flexibility is limited
  • Standard sphere ranges: −12 to +6/+8 D for most dailies; CooperVision Biofinity XR extends to −20/+15 D
  • Toric cylinder: −0.75 to −2.25 or −2.75 D in most families; axis availability restricted above −6 D at higher cylinders
  • Apply vertex conversion when spectacle Rx exceeds ±4.00 D
  • Also see: Toric Contact Lens Fitting Guide
